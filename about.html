About Me

MY BACKGROUND

Serving Our Community

Serving Chanhassen has been one of the greatest privileges of my life, and I remain grateful for every opportunity to give back to this community. Over the years, I've had the opportunity to serve as Mayor, a City Council member, and an active volunteer in our community.

Those experiences have taught me the importance of listening carefully, respecting different viewpoints, and making thoughtful decisions that put Chanhassen first.

Retirement has given me the opportunity to once again dedicate my time and energy to public service. I'm ready to bring experience, preparation, and a collaborative approach back to the City Council.
